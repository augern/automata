Automata and Parsing Tools

Interactive companions for the study of formal languages, automata and parsing. Each tool exposes the stages of a classical construction or algorithm for inspection and stepwise execution, and lets the reader test recognition of input strings and examine the resulting derivations and parse trees.

Regular languages
NFA Simulator Nondeterministic finite automata with ε-transitions  —  transition table and state graph, and the computation tree of a nondeterministic run Builds on: the idea of a finite automaton (states, transitions, DFAs) Regular Expressions Thompson's construction, subset construction and DFA minimisation  —  the translation from a regular expression to an NFA, a DFA and its minimal form, with simulation of string recognition Builds on: NFA Simulator
Context-free languages
Grammar Derivations Derivations in grammars of types 0, 1 and 2  —  construct a derivation by choosing a production and the substring it rewrites, or sample one at random Builds on: context-free grammars Grammar Transformations Normalisation of context-free grammars  —  removal of useless symbols, ε-productions, unit productions and left recursion; conversion to Chomsky normal form Builds on: context-free grammars PDA Simulator Nondeterministic pushdown automata  —  transition relation and state graph, acceptance by final state or empty stack, and the evolution of the set of reachable configurations Builds on: NFA Simulator, Grammar Derivations LL(1) Parser Top-down predictive parsing  —  computation of FIRST and FOLLOW sets, the parsing table M[A, a] and LL(1) conflicts, with a traced leftmost derivation and parse tree Builds on: Grammar Derivations; Grammar Transformations removes the left recursion LL(1) cannot handle LR Parser Bottom-up shift–reduce parsing  —  LR(0), SLR(1), LALR(1) and LR(1) item-set automata, ACTION and GOTO tables and their conflicts, with a traced rightmost derivation and parse tree Builds on: LL(1) Parser, NFA Simulator (the automaton of items)
Recursively enumerable languages
Turing Machine Deterministic single-tape Turing machines  —  transition function, tape and head, and stepwise execution of the computation Builds on: PDA Simulator
